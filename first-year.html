First Year

Six Wins

A $25m bookings year, worked back to the meetings it needs and forward through the first 180 days.

The book

Two Sources

60% of the target from existing Wipro accounts, the rest from new logos.

Expand

$15m from accounts where Wipro already sells

About 5 wins at $3m each, at a 40% win rate. Wipro knows the CIO in these accounts. DX reaches the CDO and CMO through the client partner.

New logo

$10m from clients with a dated trigger

About 1 win at $10m, at a 20% win rate. Separations, mergers and platform deadlines give a reason to change supplier.

Large deal

One pursuit of $20m or more by day 180

Shaped through the deal ladder with a Wipro leadership sponsor. A large win in year one lifts the target; the plan does not depend on it.

Win rates, deal sizes and the 4 meetings per qualified opportunity are my working assumptions, not Wipro data. Change any of them on Portfolio Maths in the appendix.

180 days

Map, Open, Shape

Three phases, each with a written output.

Days 1 to 30

Map the matrix

Meet the Americas 1 consumer and health leaders and the Americas 2 banking and manufacturing leaders, then the fifteen client partners whose accounts fall in my portfolio. Agree three to five joint accounts with each.

Output A written account plan for 25 named accounts, a dated trigger list and agreed credit rules with each client partner.

Days 31 to 90

Open conversations

Publish three short points of view tied to dated triggers: AEM 6.5 migration for medical device makers, accessibility for health and life sciences ahead of May 2027, and agent-ready commerce for retail and consumer brands.

Output Qualified pipeline of about $40m, two diagnostics in proposal and one booking.

Days 91 to 180

Shape large deals

Shape two pursuits of more than $20m TCV through the deal ladder, each with a Wipro leadership sponsor and a written plan for credit and delivery.

Output Two large pursuits in solution stage, coverage at three times, and bookings tracking to the annual target.

Every action and milestone is on First 180 Days in the appendix.

Support

Four Asks

What the plan needs from Wipro in the first two weeks.

Named client partners

An agreed list of Americas 1 and Americas 2 accounts with a named client partner and an agreed credit rule for each.

Designit time

Access to Designit strategists for two diagnostics in the first quarter, priced as a sales investment if necessary.

Solution architects

A named pre-sales architect for Adobe and one for commerce, so that first pursuits are technically sound from the first meeting.

Alliance registration

Early registration of opportunities with Adobe, Salesforce and SAP, so vendor co-sell support and funding are available.
