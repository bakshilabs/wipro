Story 1 · Foster + Partners, 2020 to 2026

A new service line inside a matrix

SituationThe firm sold buildings through design studios that owned each client. Clients were asking for advice that came before a building: environmental strategy, digital transformation and how to use their space.

What I didCo-created a strategic advisory service line and sold it into clients that the studios owned. I had no accounts of my own, so every opportunity started by giving a studio partner a reason to bring me in.

ResultThe service line took the firm into corporate environmental strategy, digital transformation and spatial consulting. As Associate Partner I led pitches and bids that won more than £10m a year in fees.

What it means at Wipro

This is the practitioner overlay. An SMU client partner owns the account, DX carries the number, and the seller earns access by growing the client partner's business.

Story 2 · Foster + Partners

Land and expand in life sciences

SituationA global pharmaceutical company wanted a single sustainability vision for its whole property portfolio, tied to its corporate commitments.

What I didPitched, negotiated and won a multi-year contract to write the sustainability vision plan for the whole portfolio, starting from an assessment of the client's own data.

ResultA multi-year contract covering the whole portfolio, for a client in one of Wipro's DX focus industries.

What it means at Wipro

The deal ladder: an assessment in the client's own numbers that opens a multi-year programme.

Story 3 · Foster + Partners and SOM

Helping the account owner win

SituationLarge competitions are won or lost in a few executive presentations. The studio partner owns the relationship but needs a specialist who can make the technical case to a chief executive.

What I didPresented to Fortune 500 chief executives for the design studios, and at SOM New York led business development for my department, presenting to executives at Wall Street banks.

ResultMy presentations helped the studios win contracts worth more than £10m. At SOM I won more than $3m a year in fees.

What it means at Wipro

Selling into Americas 2 banking and Americas 1 health accounts alongside a client partner, with the specialist case made to a CDO or CMO.
