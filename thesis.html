Thesis

Three Claims

What I think the role is for. I would like to hear where you see it differently.

Claim 1

Growth

Claim 1 · Growth

60% of revenue

Wipro needs new Americas pipeline

The Americas carry 60% of Wipro's revenue, and both Americas units shrank year on year in the latest quarter while Europe and APMEA grew. Wipro posted four US DX seller roles between July and October 2026, one per industry cluster. I read that as a bet that DX can open new conversations in accounts where Wipro already runs IT.

Operating Matrix

Four US DX seller postings, July to October 2026.

Claim 2

Gap

Claim 2 · Gap

6% creative staff

The weakness is the front end

Gartner places Wipro as a Niche Player in DX Services. The stated reasons are a thin creative bench (6% of DX staff against a market average of about 9%) and a delivery base that is about 80% in APAC. Everest rates Wipro a Leader in Salesforce services. Wipro loses ground at the first conversation with a CMO or CDO, which needs design-led people in the client's time zone.

Claim 3

Timing

Claim 3 · Timing

2027

2027 gives buyers a date

Adobe Experience Manager 6.5 on-premises support ends in February 2027. The ADA Title II and HHS Section 504 web accessibility rules take effect in April and May 2027, and Sitecore 10.4 support ends in December 2027. These dates fall on Wipro's focus industries and turn a capability pitch into a decision with a deadline.
