Argument

Why a design-led seller fits Wipro now

The gap Gartner names

Gartner's 2025 DX Services report places Wipro as a Niche Player and cautions that 6% of its DX staff are creative and about 80% are in APAC. Wipro has the delivery scale. It is short of onshore people who lead the design and advisory conversation.

What I bring to it

I trained as an architect and computational designer and have spent eighteen years in design-led consulting in New York and London, sixteen of them selling to executives. I can open the conversation the way Designit would, then hand to Wipro's delivery model with a credible plan.

What I still need

Depth on DX platform services: Adobe, Salesforce, SAP Commerce and Pega. Wipro has that depth in its practices, and partner enablement and paired pursuits close it in my first two quarters.
