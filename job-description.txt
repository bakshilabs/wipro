- Ensure that day-to-day operating requirements are met effectively, and performance objectives are achieved.
- Leverage Wipro leadership to forge strategic relationships with clients.

Experience
- Minimum 10-15 years of total experience within a professional services or technology organization with a demonstrable track record of delivering results.
- Minimum 5-7 years of experience selling digital experience technology solutions to C-Suite client stakeholders across North America.
- Consultative selling on Digital experience solutions.
- Experience in leading large experience transformation deals. Large deal min. $20m

- Deep understanding of industry & technology market trends around Experience Technologies which includes experience technologies strategy, experience design, engagement platforms, digital marketing & commerce, MarTech solutions, mobility solutions, process automation, Low Code No Code development, UI/UX design & development, Enterprise Content Mgt (ECM) and new age digital experiences like metaverse, AR/VR.
