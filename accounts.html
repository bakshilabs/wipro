Three ways into an account

Relationships I bring

At Foster + Partners, SOM and WSP I sold to executives at Roche, Genentech, JPMorgan Chase, Citigroup, Hyundai, ESPN, Apple, Oracle and Alibaba. Those relationships sat with real-estate and workplace leaders. In most cases they can introduce me to the digital and marketing leaders, but they do not replace the Wipro client partner.

Wipro clients to expand

Wipro already works for Citi, Levi Strauss, HanesBrands, TruStage, Entrust and Integra LifeSciences in the Americas. Expansion inside these accounts closes faster and at a higher win rate, so it carries the larger share of the first-year number.

New logos with a deadline

Separations and mergers give fixed dates: transition services agreements expire, brands must launch, sites must move. DePuy Synthes, Kenvue, MiniMed and the Walgreens units each have a digital estate that has to exist by a known date.
