Sample Deal

DePuy Synthes

How I would take one account from a dated trigger to a $20m pursuit. Built from public sources. The numbers are my assumptions.

Account

The Trigger

A fixed exit date that the company cannot move on its own.

Trigger

18 to 24

months to separate, from 14 October 2025

Johnson & Johnson announced its intent to separate its orthopaedics business as a standalone company named DePuy Synthes, with about $9bn of revenue. That puts completion between April and October 2027.

What it needs

A digital estate of its own

A new brand site in every market, HCP and surgeon portals, ordering and commerce, martech and customer data, all moved off J&J shared services before the transition agreements end.

The earliest completion date is 170 days away.

Buyers

Three people

The Worldwide President, named when the separation was announced. The separation CIO or CDO, who owns the cutover. The CMO, who owns the brand and the surgeon experience.

Why Wipro

Run economics at scale

A standalone company has to carry its own run costs from day one. A managed run with offshore content operations is where Wipro is cheaper than the design-led rivals, and Designit gives it the front end they would otherwise win on.

Sources: J&J press release, 14 Oct 2025; Business Wire

Ladder

Three Phases

A diagnostic small enough to buy quickly, then build and run. The run is 60% of the value.

Phase 1 · $0.7m

Diagnostic, experience strategy and design

Twelve weeks. An inventory of every site, HCP portal, ordering flow and martech tool that runs on J&J shared services, a target estate, a platform decision and a cutover plan set against the separation dates.

12 weeks, 15 people on average, 50% onshore.

Phase 2 · $7.9m

Platform build and migration

Twelve months. Stand up the new estate, migrate content and customer data, rebuild the HCP portal and surgeon ordering journeys, and meet the 2027 accessibility rules at launch.

12 months, 60 people on average, 25% onshore.

Phase 3 · $12.7m

Managed run, content operations and AI agents

Three years. Platform support, content operations across markets, testing and personalisation, and AI service agents for surgeons and hospital buyers, under service levels.

3 years, 40 people on average, 15% onshore.

Rates of $170 an hour onshore and $35 offshore at 160 hours a month are my assumptions. Change them on Pursuit Model in the appendix.

Matrix

Who Does What

Agreed with the client partner before the first buyer meeting.

Me

Originate the pursuit, write the point of view, lead the buyer conversations and the proposal, and carry the DX number.

Americas 1 client partner

Sponsor the pursuit, open the J&J and DePuy Synthes relationships, and agree the credit split.

Designit

Lead the twelve-week diagnostic, the journey research with surgeons and hospital buyers, and the target experience.

Platform practice

Own the platform decision, the build estimate and the architects on the proposal.

Delivery and run

Price and staff the managed run, and stand behind the service levels.

Alliances

Register the deal with the platform vendor and draw on co-sell funding for the diagnostic.

Deal desk and sponsor

Approve price and risk at the deal reviews, and put a Wipro executive in front of the Worldwide President.

Risks

What Could Stop It

And what I would do about each.

J&J keeps the work with its incumbents

Separation advisors and existing agencies are already inside. I would go in through the customer-facing estate, which the ERP and finance advisors do not cover, and offer to work alongside them.

The separation date moves

The ladder still holds if the dates slip, because the diagnostic is useful on any timeline. I would price it so that it stands alone.

Wipro has no footprint at J&J

Then the score stays below 70 and the right call is a smaller entry, or a pass in favour of MiniMed or a Walgreens unit, which share the same separation pattern.
