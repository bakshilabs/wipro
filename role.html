An overlay seller who originates large experience deals inside accounts that other people own.

Eleven Lines

Each line of the posting, and what I would do about it.

Line 1

“Sales Practitioner”

I would Treat every client partner as my first customer.

Line 2

“Matrixed and leveraged organization”

I would Agree credit splits before the pursuit starts.

Line 3

“Order bookings (ACV / TCV)”

I would Shape deals with a run phase: large TCV, steady ACV.

Line 4

“Large deal min. $20m”

I would Land with a small design phase, ladder to build and run.

What the posting saysWhat it means at WiproHow I would work it
As a Sales PractitionerWipro uses practitioner for an offering specialist who sells one service line into accounts that a Strategic Market Unit (SMU) client partner owns. The practitioner carries a DX number but does not own the client relationship or the account P&L.Treat every SMU client partner as my first customer. Arrive with a reason for them to bring me into their account: a dated trigger, a point of view and a sized opportunity that grows their number as well as mine.
Matrixed and leveraged organization construct as a part of the Enterprise Apps portfolioDX sits inside the Enterprise Apps portfolio, next to the platform practices (SAP, Oracle, Salesforce and others). Many DX deals are won on a platform decision, and the credit for them is shared between the SMU, the platform practice and DX.Agree credit splits and roles before the pursuit starts. Build pursuits that pull platform work through DX, so the platform practices gain from inviting me in.
Accountable for meeting sales KPIs based on Revenue growth and Order bookings (ACV / TCV)Two measures run in parallel. Total contract value (TCV) counts the whole multi-year commitment at signature. Annual contract value (ACV) and revenue show how fast it turns into billing. A three-year $21m deal is $7m of ACV a year.Shape deals with a run phase so that TCV is large and ACV is steady. Track bookings and revenue separately in my own forecast, because a design-heavy year can book well and bill late.
Experience in leading large experience transformation deals. Large deal min. $20mExperience design and build projects on their own rarely reach $20m. Deals of that size are put together: strategy and design, a platform rebuild, then multi-year managed operations of the platform, content and AI agents. Wipro's quarterly results count large deals from $30m TCV, so a $30m deal is also visible to the group.Land with a paid diagnostic or design sprint, then ladder to build and run. The Pursuit Model page shows how a $1.2m design engagement becomes a deal of more than $20m TCV.
Experience in managing portfolio of $20m to $25mThe seller is expected to hold a book of business of that size across several accounts, so the role mixes expanding existing DX work with winning new logos.Split the book into protect, expand and new logo targets from month one. The Portfolio Maths page sets the pipeline coverage each needs.
Engage with customer CDOs / CXOs ... to influence their digital experience strategyThe buyer is a business executive (chief digital officer, chief marketing officer, chief customer officer) with the CIO as co-signer. The pitch has to be about revenue, retention and cost to serve, with technology as the means.Lead with a measured business case: conversion, cost per contact, content cost per asset, time to market. Bring the CIO in early so that the IT side backs the case instead of blocking it.
Collaborate with various Wipro teams to develop proactive proposalsProactive means unsolicited. Wipro wants deals shaped before an RFP exists, when there is no competitive bake-off and the client buys the idea before it buys a supplier.Run a trigger calendar (platform end-of-support dates, regulation deadlines, leadership changes) and turn each trigger into a costed proposal before the client goes to market.
Provide thought leadership throughout the sales lifecycleThe seller is expected to bring insight that a client cannot get from its incumbent agency, and to keep bringing it after the first meeting.Publish short points of view tied to a client's own data: an accessibility scan of their site, a tech-stack audit, a benchmark against peers. I did this for net zero with carbon baselines, and the method carries over.
Drive operational efficiency and minimize friction in sales/solution/delivery processWipro wins on blended delivery with large offshore teams. A deal sold without a delivery plan turns into a margin problem for the account.Bring the delivery lead into the pursuit at qualification, price on a realistic onshore and offshore mix, and hand over with a written plan for the first 90 days.
Leverage Wipro leadership to forge strategic relationships with clientsExecutive sponsorship is a deliberate lever: a Wipro executive meets the client's executive at a decisive moment of the pursuit.Plan one leadership touchpoint per large pursuit, with a brief, a specific ask and a follow-up owner.
New age digital experiences like metaverse, AR/VRThis line reads like older boilerplate. The August and September 2026 DX postings replace it with AI and generative AI, and names Adobe, SAP Commerce, Adobe Commerce, Shopify, Pega and Appian.Lead with agentic and AI-assisted experience work, where client budgets are moving in 2026, and keep AR/VR for the few clients who have a real case for it.

Sources: Wipro careers, Director - Digital Experience (DX), posted 16 Jul 2026
