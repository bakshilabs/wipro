# DX Buying Triggers 2026-2028: Dated, Verifiable Signals for Large Digital Experience Deals

Prepared 23 October 2026 for the Wipro Director, Digital Experience (Americas) application.

**How to read this:**
- **[V]** means I checked the date against a primary source (the vendor's own page, the Federal Register, an official press release or the official policy page).
- **[S]** means the date comes from consistent secondary sources only, such as a consultancy, a law firm or trade press.
- **[U]** means unverified or conflicting. Confirm it before you quote it.
- Dates that have already passed (before 23 Oct 2026) still count. The installed base that missed the deadline is now running unsupported, which makes the urgency stronger.

## 6. Gaps and caveats
- **Adobe Campaign v7:** the official matrix (2030-12-31) conflicts with the consultancy date (2027-03-31). Present the matrix date and say "check your contract".
- **SAP Commerce 2205 (2026-07-31):** consistent across sources, but I could not reach an SAP primary page.
- **Salesforce SFRA/PWA Kit and Marketing Cloud:** no official retirement dates found. Do not claim one.
- **Optimizely CMS 12:** no fixed end date. Support ends when CMS 14 ships.
- **Alabama, Vermont and Delaware privacy dates:** from Covington's summary only (Delaware possibly unsigned).
- **Retail media figures:** paywalled. Forecasts have been revised down.
- I left out a third-party claim that Oracle Java 11 support ends in Sep 2026, because it is unverified.
