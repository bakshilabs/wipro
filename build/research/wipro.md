# Wipro corporate context for "Director - Digital Experience (DX)" (req 190063)

Research date: 23 Oct 2026. Every fact carries [source, date]. "Unverified" marks anything I could not confirm from a primary or reliable secondary source. "Inference" marks my own reading of the facts.

## 0. Key takeaways for the application

1. **Where the role sits (inference, but well supported).** The DX practice belongs to **Enterprise Applications**, which is part of the **Technology Services GBL**. The GBL has been led since 1 Apr 2026 by **Kanwar Singh**, ex-Accenture. Enterprise Applications has been led since May 2026 by **Ashish Kumar Goyal**, SVP and Global Head, also ex-Accenture. The global DX practice head is **Rajesh Damodaran** (London). The role sells "overlay" into the four SMUs. Americas 1 and Americas 2 together make up about 60% of revenue.
2. **Timing.** The Americas are the weak spot: A1 was -2.7% YoY CC and A2 -4.7% YoY CC in Q2 FY27. Large-deal TCV fell 58.6% QoQ in Q2 FY27, and Q3 guidance is -1.5% to +0.5%. Management describes discretionary spend as slower, with cost-takeout savings being "reinvested in AI". A DX seller has to pitch DX as AI-led, consulting-led and self-funding, not as discretionary UX work.
3. **Likely backfill (inference).** **Sanjeev Dubey** was "Head of Digital Experience for Americas (BFSI, Capital Mkts, HiTech, Mfg, Energy, Resources, Canada)", which is the Americas 2 sector set. His LinkedIn says he left Wipro in Jun 2026 for Infosys. Req 190063 was posted on 16 Jul 2026. Within weeks Wipro also posted three more US DX Practice Director reqs (EMR 190268, Retail/LS/MedDevices 198032, "Enterprise Experience Lead").
4. **Analyst gaps to address honestly.** Wipro was a **Niche Player** in the Gartner MQ for Digital Experience Services in both 2024 and Oct 2025. Gartner's 2025 cautions: only 6% of DX FTEs are creative versus a 9% market average, and about 80% of DX FTEs are in APAC. Wipro was a **Major Contender, not a Leader**, in the Everest Adobe Services PEAK 2025. Its strengths are Salesforce (ISG Leader in all 3 US quadrants, 2026), ServiceNow (Everest Leader and Star Performer, 2025) and Pega (Global Elite).
5. **Leadership churn and ex-Accenture hires.** The CEO and Chief Growth Officer (until 2023), Kanwar Singh, Ashish Goyal and Amit Kumar (Consulting) all came from Accenture. The Americas 2 CEO resigned effective 3 May 2026, and I found no public successor (unverified).

## 1. Operating structure (2026)

### 1.1 Matrix: four SMUs, four GBLs and two business units
- The FY2025-26 Integrated Annual Report describes the structure this way. There are four Global Business Lines (Consulting, Technology Services, Engineering, Business Process Services) and two business units (the AI-Native Business & Platforms Unit, and Capco). Four SMUs (Americas 1, Americas 2, Europe, APMEA) are "the primary axis of how we operate", responsible for client relationships, sector relevance and growth. [https://www.wipro.com/content/dam/nexus/staticsites/annual-report-2025/pdf/Integrated-annual-report-2025-26.pdf, FY2025-26 annual report, published mid-2026; exact date unverified]
- SMU definitions are from the Q2 FY27 press release:
  - **Americas 1 (US, LatAm, Canada):** Communication, Media and Networks; Technology Software and Gaming; Technology New Age; Health; Consumer.
  - **Americas 2:** Banking and Financial Services; Energy, Manufacturing and Resources; Capital Markets and Insurance.
  - **Europe:** UK and Ireland, Switzerland, Germany, Western Europe.
  - **APMEA:** ANZ, SE Asia, Japan, India, Middle East, Africa.
  - **Effective 1 Apr 2026:** LatAm and Canada customers were realigned into the A1/A2 sectors. Hi-tech and airports were subsumed into A1, and prior periods were restated.
  - Revenue is attributed to an SMU by the client's primary buying centre.
  - [https://www.wipro.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/wipro-announces-results-for-the-quarter-ended-september-30-2026/, 15 Oct 2026]
- **GBL realignment effective 1 Apr 2025.**
  - Technology Services (led by Nagendra Bandaru) covers cloud, digital services for industry, cybersecurity, AI, **enterprise applications** and infrastructure.
  - BPS is led by Jasjit Singh Kang, Consulting by Amit Kumar and Engineering by Srikumar Rao. Capco stays under Anne-Marie Rowland.
  - Jo Debecker, head of FullStride Cloud, left.
  - [https://indiainfoline.com/news/business/wipro-restructures-business-lines-to-boost-ai-cloud-and-digital-transformation, 17 Mar 2025]
- **History.** The 2023 structure had four GBLs: Wipro FullStride Cloud, Wipro Engineering Edge, Wipro Enterprise Futuring (Bandaru: data, application platforms, digital operations, cyber) and Wipro Consulting (with Capco and Designit). This followed the "One Wipro" focus. [https://www.consultancy.in/news/3931/wipro-rejigs-service-offerings-into-four-global-business-lines, 5 Mar 2023]
- Wipro Enterprise Futuring under Bandaru included Digital Operations & Platforms, Cybersecurity & Risk, Enterprise Applications, and Data, Analytics & Intelligence, plus global practices of **Experience** and AI. It had about 135,000 associates and $4.65B revenue. [https://events.reutersevents.com/momentum/studio-interview/wipro, undated, c. 2024; unverified date]
  - Inference: Enterprise Futuring was folded into Technology Services in Apr 2025. The "Enterprise Futuring" label appears to be retired.
- **AI-Native Business & Platforms Unit**, launched 1 Apr 2026, is led by Nagendra Bandaru.
  - It owns the Wipro Intelligence platform assets: NetOxygen, CROAMIS, IHS/HPS, Enterprise Telco AI, WINGS and WEGA.
  - Its stated aim is a "services-as-software" model with an invest-build-partner approach alongside Wipro Ventures.
  - [https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/companies/wipro-announces-ai-native-business-reshuffles-leadership/article70812081.ece, 1 Apr 2026]
  - It had more than 2,000 engineers, including FDEs, by Q2 FY27. [https://www.wipro.com/content/dam/nexus/en/investor/quarterly-results/2026-2027/q2fy27/q2fy27-earnings-transcript.pdf, 15 Oct 2026]

## 3. Strategy, AI offerings, acquisitions and partners

### 3.1 Strategy framing
- Wipro's stated strategy is "Consulting-led. AI-powered. Industry-focused. Client-centric. Purpose-driven." Its priorities include "Sourcing, Shaping & winning large deals with Consulting-led, AI powered approach" and "Building Large Accounts". [https://www.wipro.com/content/dam/nexus/en/investor/quarterly-results/2025-2026/q4fy26/wipro-investor-ppt-q4fy26.pdf, Apr 2026]
- **Wipro Intelligence™** launched in Oct 2025 with Q2 FY26 results. It is a "unified suite of AI platforms, solutions, and transformative offerings" and includes the Wipro Innovation Network (labs, partners, start-ups, academia). [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/srini-pallia_i-am-pleased-to-introducewipro-intelligence-activity-7386520940966371328-WugX, 21 Oct 2025; https://money.rediff.com/news/market/wipro-q2-profit-up-launches-wipro-intelligence/35461120251016, 16 Oct 2025]
  - At that time Pallia said "Discretionary spend is moving more and more into AI-related projects." [Rediff/PTI, 16 Oct 2025]
- **Wipro Intelligence stack, as Pallia put it on the Q2 FY27 call:**
  - "Build AI": WEGA, covering the SDLC and product development lifecycle.
  - "Run AI": WINGS, covering run and operate.
  - "Reimagine AI": AI consulting and advisory, new AI services, and the AI-Native Unit's industry platforms.
  - [Q2 FY27 transcript, 15 Oct 2026]
- The **"dual engine" model**, core services plus AI-native platforms, aims at "services-as-a-software" and "nonlinear growth". [Q4 FY26 transcript, 16 Apr 2026]
- HFS profiles Wipro's CX model as "consulting-led, AI-embedded... anchored on its proprietary agentic platforms, WEGA and the **Wipro AI Experience Studio**". [https://www.hfsresearch.com/research/wipro-customer-experience-services-capabilities-2026/, 13 Aug 2026]

### 3.2 Designit and Capco
- Designit is Wipro's "experience innovation company", acquired in Jul 2015 for about DKK 635M ($94M). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Designit, May 2026]

## 6. How the sales organisation works

### 6.1 Role taxonomy (from postings and filings)
- **SMU / account side.** Client Partners, Global Account Executives (GAEs) and Account Executives (AEs) own accounts. "Hunters" chase new logos; "farmers" expand existing accounts.
  - The 2026 BPS practitioner req says to "Partner with GAEs/AEs as the primary entry point to buyers". [https://lensa.com/job-v1/wipro/minneapolis-mn/client-partner/7b056ad9331370b8f707bdc13899f7ed, posted 26 May / 8 Jun 2026]
  - The Cloud & Digital practitioner req says: "Work collaboratively with Client Partners and Global Account Executives in a matrix model to sell into their accounts". It is "the technical deal owner", and its KPIs are new bookings (TCV and ACV), profitable revenue, and a monthly forecast within 5% variance. That req asks for a $15–20M portfolio. [https://careers.wipro.com/job/Cloud-&-Digital-Director-Automotive/183342-en_US/, 2026]
- **Practitioner ("Practitioner Sales", PR or PSM)** is the service-line overlay seller. It is "accountable for developing global accounts for the Service Line... works with a set of Enterprise Business Managers in a cross-functional manner to achieve Service Line targets". [https://careers.wipro.com/job/BPS-Sales-Director-%28MFD%29/126953-en_US, posted 30 Jan 2026; https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/practitioner-sales-manager-at-wipro-219401813, c. 2018]
- **The DX practitioner (req 190063)** owns Experience Technologies sales in the Americas. Its KPIs are revenue growth and order bookings (ACV/TCV) in "a defined portfolio". It works "in a matrixed and leveraged organization construct as part of the Enterprise Apps portfolio" with the SMUs, Wipro sales and the Experience Technologies practices. It expects large deals of at least $20M and a $20–25M portfolio, with pay of $200–280k. [req 190063, 16 Jul 2026]
- **A public view of the sales incentive plan (SIP)** comes from a UK Employment Tribunal judgment, *Chandrashekarappa v Wipro*. The judgment is dated around 2024; the date is unverified, and the claims were filed in 2022.
  - Wipro "categorised sales work as either 'hunting' or 'farming'". Hunting (new logo, "new new") is higher risk and higher reward.
  - Practitioner sales staff are technical experts who support the lead hunter. "The primary 'hunter' might work with the client's CIO, those in practitioner sales would typically focus on work with individual managers or specialists."
  - The H2 SIP said practitioner sales hunters in CIS and DOP "can also receive up to 1% of invoiced revenue for hunting account... maximum payout USD 150k p.a." (clause 6.5.3).
  - Practitioner role names include "Practitioner Sales Farmer" and "Practitioner Sales Hunter Manager".
  - [https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/668beac24a94d44125d9ce95/Mr_P_Chandrashekarappa_v_Wipro_Ltd-3300028-2022_and_3311832-2022-FMH_Reserved_Judgment.pdf, judgment c. Jul 2024]
- **Consulting partners** carry revenue and order-book targets, utilisation, and "pull-through revenue for wider Wipro services". [https://getmereferred.com/job-listing/partner-tech-impl-l1-wipro-pune-2-to-5-years-experience-ed1a2960-3578-4cd7-959c-ff3c99e20da9, 13 Aug 2025]
- **Large deals:** Wipro reports large deals at ≥$30M TCV. [press releases] The DX req uses a "$20M minimum" large-deal threshold. The Germany BD Director reqs ask for "closing large multi technology deals 30Mn USD and above". [https://careers.wipro.com/job/Business-Development-Director-%28MFD%29/212298-en_US/, 20 Oct 2026]
- **Chief Growth Office history.**
  - Created by Delaporte in 2020–21 to centralise large-deal pursuit (solution architects, pricing, sales excellence, partners, marketing). The SMU reorganisation took effect 1 Jan 2021.
  - About 75 SVPs and 300 GMs reportedly exited in year one. [https://tacticalvc.ai/cases/wipro-sales-restructuring-chief-growth-office, 6 May 2026; secondary analysis]
  - Trautman says she launched "Elevate", a sales transformation programme. [https://linkedin.com/in/stephanie-trautman, crawled Oct 2026]
  - Strategic pursuits moved into the SMUs in 2023. [CNBC TV18, 8 Dec 2023]
  - Some "Chief Growth Office" titles persist in the field, for example a GM for Tech Partnerships, UKI & Europe. [https://linkedin.com/in/shehbaz-singh-jaggi, crawled 15 Oct 2026]
- **"One Wipro"** was the 2023 framing for coordinating acquisitions and business lines. [Consultancy.in, 5 Mar 2023] It sits alongside the current "client-centric... SMUs are the primary axis" operating model. [Annual Report FY25-26]
