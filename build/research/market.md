# Digital Experience (DX) Services: Americas Market and Competitor Picture

Prepared 23 Oct 2026 for the Wipro Director, Digital Experience Sales (Americas) application.

**Conventions**
- Every fact gives a source URL and the publication date (or "n.d." when the page carries no date).
- **[UNVERIFIED]** means the fact comes from a secondary or aggregator source, sources conflict, or the claim could not be checked against a primary source.
- **[SECONDARY-GARTNER]** means Gartner MQ text reproduced by a third-party site (research.oz.spotlightar.com). The placements were cross-checked against a Gartner graphic hosted by Accenture and against vendor press releases where possible.

## 0. Key takeaways for a Wipro DX seller (synthesis, not sourced facts)

1. **Wipro's analyst position in DX is weak.** In the 2025 Gartner MQ for DX Services Wipro is a **Niche Player**. Infosys, IBM and HCLTech are Challengers, and the Leaders are Accenture, Capgemini, Deloitte, Dentsu and Publicis Sapient. Everest Adobe Services 2025 places Wipro as a **Major Contender**, against TCS, Infosys and IBM as Leaders. Wipro's strongest DX-adjacent rating is **Everest Salesforce Services 2024, Leader**.
2. **Gartner's main criticisms of Wipro** are: a low creative share (6% of DX FTEs against a 9% average), about 80% of DX FTEs in APAC, and a focus on deals above $5M. Gartner credits its scale (65,000+ DX FTEs, the largest headcount figure in the MQ excerpts), certification, more than $1B in AI investment and repeat business. The Americas pitch therefore needs onshore and nearshore design proof (Designit studios) and run/managed DX at scale.
3. **Budgets are flat but shifting.** Marketing spend is 7.8% of revenue in 2026. The martech share fell to 19.4% and the agency share to 19.2%, while labour rose to 24.5% and AI took 15.3%. This favours consolidated, AI-led "marketing operations as a service" deals over agency-of-record or one-off build work.
4. **Pricing is deflating.** Analysts estimate 2–5% AI-led deflation of build/run revenue, and renewals carry productivity pass-through. Outcome-based contracts are about 12% of DX revenue on average (Gartner), and IDC expects 30% of IT services contracts to be outcome-based by 2029. Large DX deals of $20M+ will increasingly be run/optimise annuities with AI productivity commitments.
5. **The platforms are going agentic.** Adobe has bought Semrush (GEO/agentic search), Salesforce has bought Informatica (Agentforce 360), Sitecore has launched SitecoreAI, Optimizely has Opal, ServiceNow has bought Moveworks, and OutSystems has Agent Workbench. Agentic commerce protocols (OpenAI ACP, Google UCP) create a new body of "agent-ready commerce" services work.

## 4. Deal structures in DX services
| Theme | Evidence | Source/date |
|---|---|---|
| Typical sizes | TCS gets 70% of DX revenue from engagements above $1M and 95% from clients with more than 2,500 employees. Wipro targets DX deals above $5M. | Run versus project | 36% of DX FTEs across the MQ cohort are in "ongoing services" (run/optimise) against 43% in implementation. IDC expects 30% of IT services contracts to be outcome-based by 2029 **[UNVERIFIED secondhand]**. | Spotlightar MQ 2025; letsdatascience 2026 |

Martech is moving to consumption pricing (Gartner CMO 2026). | Globant PR 5 Jun 2025; https://www.bain.com/es/insights/ai-pods-as-a-service-modular-scalable-and-built-for-speed/ ; MediaPost 11 May 2026 |
