# Wipro DX Americas: Account Research (as of 23 Oct 2026)

Scope: (1) the candidate's existing relationship accounts, (2) new-logo North American enterprises with 2025-26 DX buying signals in Wipro's DX focus industries (Retail & Consumer, Life Sciences, MedTech) plus Banking and Automotive, (3) Wipro's known Americas clients, so the matrix can show "expand" vs "new logo".

Conventions: every fact has a source URL and date. **[UNVERIFIED]** means I did not confirm it in a primary or reputable secondary source during this research. Revenue figures marked "approx." come from general knowledge of public filings and were not re-checked here. Verify them before you use them externally. Where no name appears, none was confirmed.
