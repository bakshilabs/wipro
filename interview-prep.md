# Interview Prep

Private notes for the one-hour meeting. This file is not linked from the site and should not be shared.

## How the site is split
The meeting track is what goes on screen: Agenda, Track Record, Thesis, First Year, Sample Deal, Questions and the CV. Each page takes five to twelve minutes and ends with three questions that hand the conversation back to the interviewer.

The research appendix holds the nine working pages (Role Anatomy, Operating Matrix, Market and Competitors, Buying Triggers, Account Map, Pursuit Model, Portfolio Maths, First 180 Days, Fit and Evidence). Open one only when a question needs it. The two calculators (Pursuit Model and Portfolio Maths) are the most useful to open live, because the interviewer can change an input and watch the answer move.

## Rules for the hour
- Show less and ask more. If a page is still on screen after its time box, move on.
- Lead every segment with the conclusion, then the evidence.
- Let the interviewer correct the Thesis. Being corrected on Wipro's internals is useful and expected.
- Raise the gaps before they do, on the Track Record page.
- Close with the offer to send a draft account plan within a week.

## Talk track
**Track Record (8 min).** Open with one sentence: "I have spent sixteen years selling design-led consulting to executives, mostly as the specialist inside someone else's account." Tell story 1 in full, stories 2 and 3 in one minute each. Finish on the gaps slide and say what I would do in the first 90 days to close the platform gap.

**Thesis (7 min).** Three claims, one minute each, then ask the first discussion question. The point to land is that the Gartner weakness is the first conversation with a CMO or CDO, and that is where my experience sits.

**First Year (12 min).** Walk the chain from $25m to 70 meetings. Say plainly that the ratios are my assumptions and invite theirs. Spend most of the time on the 180 days and the four asks. Ask how the book is handed over.

**Sample Deal (10 min).** Trigger, ladder, score, matrix. The message is method: a dated event, a small paid entry, and a run phase that makes it large. Be clear that Wipro's J&J relationship is unconfirmed and that the score depends on it.

**Their questions (13 min).** Answer briefly, then offer the appendix page if it helps.

**My questions (5 min).** Ask the "book" questions first, and always ask about next steps and timing.

## Likely questions and short answers
**"You have not sold DX platforms. Why should we take the risk?"** The weakness analysts name in Wipro's DX business is the opening conversation, and that is the part I have done for sixteen years. Platform depth is Wipro's strength and I would pair with a DX architect on every early pursuit while I earn Adobe and Salesforce sales accreditations in the first 90 days.

**"Have you closed a $20m deal?"** Not as a single services contract. I have won more than £10m a year in fees and multi-year contracts, and the Sample Deal shows how I would build a $20m pursuit from a sub-$1m diagnostic plus build and run, with Wipro's deal desk and a leadership sponsor.

**"How would you work with client partners who own the accounts?"** The same way I worked at Foster + Partners: arrive with a reason for them to bring me in, a dated trigger and a sized opportunity that grows their number, and agree the credit split before the pursuit starts.

**"Why leave architecture for IT services sales?"** My work moved from buildings toward digital transformation, spatial consulting and AI training. DX is where design and technology are bought together, at a scale Wipro can deliver.

**"Where would you be based?"** Confirm before the meeting: willingness to relocate and the preferred US city. The posting lists Detroit, and other copies list Chicago.

**"What would you do in the first 30 days?"** Map the matrix: meet the client partners, agree three to five joint accounts with each, learn the offers and rate cards, meet the platform partner managers, and clean the inherited pipeline.

## Check before the meeting
- Gartner MQ placements against the Gartner document rather than the secondary reproduction.
- Whether Wipro has a current relationship at J&J, and who leads Wipro's carve-out work.
- Names and titles of Wipro leaders on LinkedIn.
- The DePuy Synthes separation timetable, in case J&J has updated it.
