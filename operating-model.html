Matrix

Operating Matrix

Wipro sells through four strategic market units that own clients and four global business lines that own offerings. The DX practice sits inside Enterprise Applications in Technology Services. This page maps where the role sits, who it depends on and what Wipro's current results ask of it.

Structure from the FY2025-26 annual report and the Q2 FY27 results of 15 October 2026.

Structure

Where the role sits

Revenue is booked to the market unit that owns the client. The DX practitioner carries a number for the practice and gets there through the market units, mostly Americas 1 for consumer and health and Americas 2 for banking and manufacturing.

What the numbers say

Both Americas units shrank year on year in Q2 FY27 while Europe and APMEA grew. Large-deal bookings fell sharply in the quarter, and guidance for Q3 is between -1.5% and +0.5%. Wipro needs new Americas pipeline, and it needs it to be large.

What the CEO says buyers want

On the Q1 FY27 call Srini Pallia said discretionary spend has been slower, decisions take longer, and clients are reinvesting cost savings in AI. A DX pitch therefore has to pay for itself and lead with AI, with experience design as the way to get the value.

What the hiring pattern says

Between July and October 2026 Wipro posted four US DX practitioner roles: this one, energy and manufacturing, retail and life sciences, and an enterprise experience lead. Wipro is building a bench of sector DX sellers across both Americas units at the same time.

What the leadership changes say

Technology Services and Enterprise Applications both have new leaders since April and May 2026, both from Accenture. New leaders tend to back a consulting-led, large-deal model and to measure sellers against it early.

Compensation logic

How practitioner selling is measured

Overlay

Practitioner sellers are specialists who support the account lead. A Wipro cloud and digital practitioner posting asks them to "work collaboratively with Client Partners and Global Account Executives in a matrix model to sell into their accounts" and to act as "the technical deal owner".

Hunting and farming

A UK employment tribunal judgment describes Wipro's sales incentive plan as dividing work into hunting (new logos) and farming (expansion), with hunting paid at higher risk and reward. The two need different plans and different pipeline coverage.

Forecast discipline

The same cloud and digital posting sets bookings (TCV and ACV), profitable revenue and a monthly forecast within 5% variance as the measures. I would expect DX to be held to the same standard.

Sources: Wipro Cloud and Digital Director posting, 2026; Chandrashekarappa v Wipro, UK Employment Tribunal, c. 2024
