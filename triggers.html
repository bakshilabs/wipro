Pattern

Same Playbook

I sold net zero transformation against deadlines. Digital experience is bought the same way.

Net zero

New York City Local Law 97 set carbon limits for large buildings from 2024, with fines per tonne over the limit. Owners bought audits, then retrofits.

Digital experience

ADA Title II from April 2027 and HHS Section 504 from May 2027 set WCAG 2.1 AA as the legal standard. Owners will buy audits, then remediation and redesign.

Net zero

The UK minimum energy efficiency standard stopped landlords letting buildings rated F or G. The building still stood but could not earn rent.

Digital experience

A platform past end of support still runs but cannot be patched. AEM 6.5 on premises reaches that point in February 2027.

Net zero

The EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive made companies publish emissions data, and the gap between pledge and performance became visible to investors.

Digital experience

AI answers reduce clicks on organic results, and the drop shows up in traffic and revenue reports that executives read.

Net zero

I won a multi-year contract to write a sustainability vision plan for a global pharmaceutical company's property portfolio, starting from a baseline of every site.

Digital experience

A scan of a client's whole digital estate (platforms, versions, accessibility, page speed) gives the same baseline, and the same route to a multi-year programme.
