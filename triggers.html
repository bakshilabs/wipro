Triggers

Buying Triggers

Large digital experience programmes start when something with a date on it forces a decision. Between now and the end of 2028 a cluster of platform support deadlines and accessibility rules lands on Wipro's focus industries. This page lists them, shows what each one forces and names the signal that reveals which clients are affected.

Dates checked against vendor support pages, the Federal Register and company releases in October 2026. The US accessibility deadlines moved back a year in April and May 2026.

Pattern

The same buying pattern I sold in net zero

Net zero transformation and digital experience transformation are bought the same way. A deadline creates urgency, a baseline assessment creates the case, and a multi-year programme follows.

Buying patterns compared
PatternIn net zero consulting, which I soldIn digital experience
A dated regulation with penaltiesNew York City Local Law 97 set carbon limits for large buildings from 2024, with fines per tonne over the limit. Owners bought audits, then retrofits.ADA Title II from April 2027 and HHS Section 504 from May 2027 set WCAG 2.1 AA as the legal standard. Owners will buy audits, then remediation and redesign.
An asset that becomes unusableThe UK minimum energy efficiency standard stopped landlords letting buildings rated F or G. The building still stood but could not earn rent.A platform past end of support still runs but cannot be patched. AEM 6.5 on premises reaches that point in February 2027.
Disclosure that exposes the gapThe EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive made companies publish emissions data, and the gap between pledge and performance became visible to investors.AI answers reduce clicks on organic results, and the drop shows up in traffic and revenue reports that executives read.
A portfolio assessment as the way inI won a multi-year contract to write a sustainability vision plan for a global pharmaceutical company's property portfolio, starting from a baseline of every site.A scan of a client's whole digital estate (platforms, versions, accessibility, page speed) gives the same baseline, and the same route to a multi-year programme.

Caveats

  • Adobe Campaign Classic v7 is left out because Adobe's support matrix gives 31 December 2030 while some consultancies cite 31 March 2027.
  • Salesforce has published no retirement date for SFRA, PWA Kit or Marketing Cloud Engagement, so none is claimed.
  • The SAP Commerce 2205 date comes from secondary sources.
  • The CMO tenure figure covers the S&P 500 and is not comparable with earlier Fortune 500 studies.
