Scorecard

Pursue, Shape or Walk Away

Pick an example or score your own. Watch the dot move.

Compelling event with a date weight 15A deadline the client cannot move: end of platform support, a regulation date, a launch, a separation.
Economic buyer engaged weight 15A CDO, CMO or chief customer officer who owns the budget has met us.
Quantified value case weight 10The benefit is in the client's own numbers: conversion, cost to serve, content cost, time to market.
Wipro account position weight 15The SMU client partner sponsors the pursuit, and Wipro already has a footprint to build on.
Platform partner alignment weight 10Adobe, Salesforce, SAP, Pega or another vendor co-sells with us on this client.
Competitive position weight 10Are we shaping the deal before an RFP, or answering one written for an incumbent?
Deliverability and margin weight 10Skills are available, the onshore and offshore mix is realistic, and margin clears the account threshold.
Run and scale potential weight 10There is a multi-year managed phase (platform, content, AI agents) that makes the TCV large.
Strategic value weight 5A reference client, a new logo in a focus industry, or a first deal on a new platform.
0Weighted score out of 100
0Urgency and right to win, each out of 100

Urgency combines the event, buyer and value scores. Right to win combines account position, platform alignment, competition and deliverability. Run potential and strategic value add to the overall score only.
