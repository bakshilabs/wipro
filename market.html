Market and Competitors

Digital experience services is a market of about $90bn among the largest providers, growing at about 6% a year. Budgets are moving from agencies and licences towards operations and AI. This page sets out the size and direction of the market, where analysts place Wipro and how I would sell against each type of competitor.

From agencies to operations

CMOs are moving money from agencies and martech licences to their own labour and to AI. Services firms that run content operations, platforms and AI agents gain from that shift. Agency-of-record creative work does not.

From projects to run contracts

Over a third of DX staff across the MQ providers work on ongoing services. Buyers want reusable accelerators, run commitments and KPIs written into the contract. That is how $20m deals get built.

From hours to outcomes

AI is pushing build prices down. Analysts estimate 2% to 5% a year of deflation, and ISG reports Americas managed services contract value down 12% in Q2 2026. The seller who prices on outcomes keeps margin when hours fall.

Sources: ISG Index Q2 2026 via MarketBeat, 10 Jul 2026

Positioning

Where Wipro can win

Wipro has the scale for multi-year run work and a design company in Designit. The commercial question for this role is how often Designit leads the front of a deal so that Wipro is bought as an experience partner.

Objections

Wipro's weak points and how I would answer them

A buyer's procurement team will read the same analyst reports. A seller who raises the weak points first, and answers them with a staffing plan, keeps control of the conversation.

Objection

Wipro is a Niche Player in the Gartner DX MQ

Gartner rates Wipro's execution on scale, investment and repeat business, and places it outside the leaders because of a small creative share and APAC-weighted delivery. That describes a capability gap at the front of the work, and Designit's studios exist to fill it.

Response. Lead with a Designit-led diagnostic and an onshore experience lead. Show the run economics only after the client trusts the design thinking.

Objection

About 80% of Wipro's DX staff are in APAC

That ratio is the reason Wipro can offer a lower total cost of ownership for multi-year run work. Clients worry about it when the onshore team is thin in the early, ambiguous phases.

Response. Staff the first phase onshore and nearshore. Move to the offshore model in build and run, with the client's agreement on the transition plan.

Objection

Only 6% of Wipro's DX staff are creative

Wipro should not compete for agency-of-record creative work. CMOs are moving budget from agencies and martech towards labour and AI, which suits a platform, content operations and AI partner.

Response. Position Wipro beside the creative agency, running the platforms, content supply chain and AI agents.

Objection

Large-deal bookings fell to $674m in Q2 FY27

Clients may read this as weak momentum, and inside Wipro it raises the value of every large pursuit in the Americas.

Response. Bring large-deal shaping skill and a pipeline of trigger-based proactive proposals in the first two quarters.
